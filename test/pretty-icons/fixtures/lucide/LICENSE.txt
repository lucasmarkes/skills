Ten icons from Lucide (https://lucide.dev), lucide-static 1.52.0, unchanged:
circle, code-xml, columns-2, hexagon, layout-grid, link-2, shield, smartphone,
square, target. They are here to show that live, gap and gravity pass on a
set drawn by others at 24.

Two of the first picks were swapped, both on gravity, because Lucide centres
an icon by its bounds and check.mjs centres it by its mass:
  triangle      -> hexagon     (mass +0, +2.32: the triangle is light on top)
  chart-column  -> columns-2   (mass -1.57, +2.58: the axis pulls it down-left)

circle, link-2, smartphone, square and target are among the icons Lucide
derives from Feather, so the Feather MIT notice below applies to them too.
